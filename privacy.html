QuoteTube Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026 · Operated by Moament

QuoteTube is an internal Windows desktop tool operated by Moament to upload short videos that Moament creates to Moament's own YouTube channels (and prepare them for TikTok). It is not offered to the public.

1. YouTube API Services

QuoteTube uses YouTube API Services. By using QuoteTube you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. YouTube data is handled under the Google Privacy Policy.

  • Authorization: the channel owner signs in with Google OAuth 2.0 (scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube).
  • Data accessed: the authorized channel's name, its uploads playlist, and the titles, view/like counts and processing status of videos on that channel.
  • Purpose: uploading and scheduling videos (title, description, tags, thumbnail, playlist), checking that an upload was processed, and avoiding duplicate uploads. Video statistics are used only to decide which videos Moament makes next.
  • QuoteTube does not access data of any other users or channels, and does not sell, share or transfer YouTube data to anyone.

2. TikTok

  • TikTok account information: open ID and display name (scope user.info.basic), used only to show which account is connected.
  • Uploads: videos sent to the connected account's TikTok inbox as drafts (scope video.upload), under the TikTok Privacy Policy.

3. Where data is stored

OAuth tokens, the upload queue and statistics are stored only on Moament's own computer. QuoteTube has no server. Data is sent only to Google/YouTube and TikTok through their official APIs.

4. Deleting data and revoking access

Removing a channel in QuoteTube deletes its stored tokens. Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings (myaccount.google.com/permissions) and, for TikTok, in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services). Locally stored statistics are refreshed regularly and deleted when a channel is removed.

5. Children

QuoteTube is not directed to children under 13.
