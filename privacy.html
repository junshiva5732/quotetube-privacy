QuoteTube Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

1. Information the app uses

  • TikTok account information: your TikTok open ID and display name (scope user.info.basic), used only to show which account is connected.
  • Access tokens: the OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok, used to upload your videos (scope video.upload).
  • Videos and captions: the video files you choose to upload, sent directly from your computer to TikTok.

2. Where data is stored

All information is stored only on your own computer. QuoteTube has no server and does not collect, sell, or share your data with anyone. Videos are transmitted only to TikTok through the official TikTok API.

3. Third parties

Uploaded content is handled by TikTok under the TikTok Privacy Policy.

4. Deleting your data

Click "Log out" in QuoteTube to delete the stored tokens from your computer, and revoke the app's access in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services).

5. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.
